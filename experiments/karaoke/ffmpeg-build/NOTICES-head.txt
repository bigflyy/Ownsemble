Notices for ffmpeg.exe (Ownsemble video-tools-1)
================================================

Ownsemble runs this ffmpeg.exe as a separate program to turn a song, its lyrics and a
background picture into a karaoke video. It is a trimmed, statically linked build that
contains the parts below. The complete corresponding source, the build scripts and the
build options are in ffmpeg-video-tools-1-sources.tar in the same release:
https://github.com/bigflyy/Ownsemble/releases/tag/video-tools-1
